Questions partners ask

Quick answers.

How does the launch offer work?

Apply through this page within 3 days of your first visit and you get 20% off the product price of your first 3 orders, placed within 12 months of your first order. The countdown on this page shows your time left. The discount is on the product price; your first pallet is delivered free anyway. One offer per company.

What are your prices?

They depend on format and volume, so we send a price list after a short call. Prices are quoted delivered to your warehouse.

Is there a minimum order?

You can start with a trial order. Volumes are agreed later.

What if a delivery is not right?

Every batch has a batch passport. If a delivery does not match it, tell us within 14 days and we replace it or refund it.

Can I get a sample first?

Yes, free. Tick the box in the application.

How much can you supply?

Up to 100 m³ a month. Demand peaks in spring and autumn, so plan those orders early.

What are the lead time and delivery?

Orders usually leave within 5 to 10 working days, by truck from Latvia to anywhere in Europe. Freight is included in the quote, and your first pallet is delivered free.

What are the payment terms?

Prepayment for the first order. Regular partners can agree flexible terms.

Can it be used in organic production?

Vermicompost is an authorised soil conditioner under Regulation (EU) 2021/1165, Annex II. We supply an organic suitability statement for your customers' certifiers.

What if growers near me contact you directly?

We pass them to you.
