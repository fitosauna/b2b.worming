Why now

Growers want less peat. Few suppliers offer quality and margin.

Substrate producers are under pressure to cut peat. Germany, for example, has a national plan to phase peat out of hobby potting soil and to reduce it in professional growing media by 2030. Garden retailers are looking for organic products their customers trust.

Most of what is on offer falls into two groups. Cheap bulk imports from outside Europe, with long shipping times and little paperwork. Or finished retail brands that leave little room for your margin and cannot carry your name.

Worming sits in between. A European producer that makes one product, keeps a record of every batch, ships from Latvia in days, and will pack it under your brand if you want.

What you would be selling

Worm humus, screened and on record.

Solid biohumus, also called vermicompost. Californian red worms process manure, vegetable matter and leaves. The result is a dark, fine, earth-like material with no smell.

  • Full production cycle on one site, from feedstock to packing
  • Sieved to an even crumb and heat-treated at 70 °C
  • Authorised for EU organic production (Regulation (EU) 2021/1165, Annex II)
  • Specification sheet and batch report with every order
  • Stores from −30 °C to +40 °C, protected from rain and sun

Questions partners ask

Answers before you apply.

What are your prices?

Prices depend on format, volume and territory, so we send a partner price list after your application and a short call. Every price is quoted delivered to your warehouse.

Is there a minimum order?

You can start with a trial order to test the product with your customers. Volume commitments come later, when we agree your territory.

Can I get exclusive rights for my country?

Yes. We grant exclusive territories. The conditions, such as yearly volume and timing, are agreed with each partner.

Do you offer private label?

Yes. We can pack retail bags under your brand. Bag sizes, design and quantities are agreed with you.

Can I get a sample first?

Yes, the sample is free. Tick the box in the application and tell us where to send it.

What are the payment terms?

Prepayment for the first order. Regular partners can agree flexible terms.

What is the lead time?

Orders are usually dispatched within 5 to 10 working days of confirmation. Delivery time depends on your country and is confirmed in each quote.

How is it delivered?

By truck from Latvia to anywhere in Europe, on pallets in big bags, as retail bags, or in bulk. Freight is included in the quote.

What documents do I get?

A product specification sheet and a batch report with each order, and an organic suitability statement for your certifier on request.

Can it be used in organic production?

Vermicompost is listed as an authorised soil conditioner in Annex II of Regulation (EU) 2021/1165. Your customers should confirm with their certifier.

What if growers in my territory contact you directly?

We pass them to you. Growers in your territory are your customers.

How is worm humus different from compost?

Compost is broken down by microbes in a heap that heats up. Worm humus has passed through worms, which leaves a fine, earth-like material with no smell. Growers use it in small amounts, often alongside compost.
